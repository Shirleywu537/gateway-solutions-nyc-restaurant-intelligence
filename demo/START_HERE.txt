GATEWAY SOLUTIONS | NYC RESTAURANT INTELLIGENCE
Offline demonstration | Prepared October 23, 2026

OPEN THE DEMO
1. Download and unzip Gateway_Solutions_Offline_Demo.zip.
2. Open Gateway_Solutions_Demo.html in a desktop browser, such as Chrome,
   Edge, or Safari. If double-clicking opens an editor, use Open With > Chrome.
3. Use the navigation to explore Home and the three research tools.

The HTML contains its own code, styles, map, and data. There is no installation,
account, API key, or server to set up. The core demo works without internet.
External source links need an internet connection.

Download the file before opening it. Email and cloud-storage previews may
show a blank page because they do not run interactive HTML. For a presentation,
use a desktop browser; mobile file-preview apps may also block JavaScript.

SUGGESTED FIVE-MINUTE WALKTHROUGH
1. Home: introduce the platform and the three questions it helps owners explore.
2. Market Investigator: switch between Restaurant presence and Weekly news
   attention, then select Bar chart or Donut chart. Open a news card or the
   methodology to inspect the evidence.
3. Location Picker: set the monthly rent budget to $12,000 and keep All boroughs.
   Select Chinese & dim sum, then click Find neighborhoods: Sunset Park leads.
   Try Japanese & sushi: Long Island City leads. Try Mexican & tacos: Mott Haven
   leads. Select map markers and compare neighborhoods with the checkboxes.
4. Risk Analyzer: choose a restaurant concept and click Analyze my risks.
   Japanese & sushi brings raw-fish handling into the concept watchlist.
   Delivery & takeout prioritizes delivery-related notices. Try changing the
   example wage from $16.50 to $17.00 and rerun the reference calculation.
5. Ask Gateway: click the lower-right assistant. Ask "Where should I open a
   sushi restaurant?" followed by "What about Chinese?" The assistant carries
   the context into its next answer and links to the relevant tool and sources.

WHAT THIS EDITION INCLUDES
- The existing English design, home page, and three tools.
- Cuisine percentages, bar and donut charts, and a saved news brief.
- Fifteen restaurant concepts and eight candidate neighborhoods.
- An interactive borough map, concept-dependent rankings, and comparison.
- Concept-specific operating checks, policy filtering, a reference wage-gap
  calculation, and local keyword screening of pasted notice text.
- A rule-based research assistant available on every page.

DATA AND LIMITS
- Restaurant metrics and neighborhood evidence: snapshot captured October 20,
  2026; the included source-record date is October 19, 2026.
- Policy feed: fourteen saved notices captured October 20, 2026. Publication
  dates are not effective dates; this is a limited selection of NYC notices.
- Curated news brief: five articles covering October 2-8, 2026.
- Operating reference rules: reviewed October 9, 2026.
- Restaurant presence and media mentions do not measure customer preference,
  revenue, or actual demand. Rents and transit scores are illustrative.
- The assistant uses included data and rules. It is not generative AI and
  cannot search the web or reliably answer arbitrary questions.
- Reload buttons read the same saved snapshot. This file has no live feed,
  background monitoring, daily refresh, or automatic policy-rule updates.
- Inputs and conversation state reset when the page is reloaded or closed.
  Questions and pasted notice text are processed locally.
